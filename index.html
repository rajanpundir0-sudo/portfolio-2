00:00:15:00 — ABOUT

DEHRADUN, INDIA

Based in the hills. Editing for screens everywhere.

I grew up and trained in Dehradun — my editing foundations were built at DSOM (Dehradun School of Media). For the last two years I've been cutting short-form reels, wedding and event highlights, and brand videos for clients who need footage turned into something people actually finish watching.

My approach: strong first-three-seconds hooks, pacing that matches the music, and color that feels intentional rather than filtered. Every project starts with the story, not the transitions.
